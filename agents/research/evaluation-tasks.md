> NVIDIA 실적이 발표됐어. 발표 내용을 분석해주고, 앞으로 메모리 주식 섹터에 미칠 영향에 대한 전문적인 분석을 부탁해.

Pass criteria:

- Current NVIDIA earnings facts are sourced and separated from interpretation.
- AI accelerator demand is connected to the memory/HBM value chain using explicit premises.
- The answer does not stop merely because no source states the final sector conclusion verbatim.
- First- and second-order effects are explained through volume, price, mix, margin, earnings, and valuation where relevant.
- Material supplier differences are discussed only where supported.
- Bull, base, and bear scenarios identify triggers and mechanisms.
- Counterarguments and already-priced-in risk are included.
- Material claims are distinguished as FACT, INFERENCE, FORECAST, or UNKNOWN.
- Inferences include confidence and assumptions; they are not labeled `NOT VERIFIED`.
- No unsupported number, event, supplier relationship, or qualification status is introduced.
